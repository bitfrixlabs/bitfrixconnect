COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT


BITFRIX (BTFX) is an initiative developed by a group of blockchain and cryptocurrency enthusiasts dedicated to building a DeFi ecosystem through the development of multi-pool rewards via staking pools. Its goal is to empower individuals with crypto portfolio management skills, enabling them to achieve their future financial objectives through the BITFRIX (BTFX) ecosystem.

In this regard, the role of a strong and mutually supportive community is very important because it is the main pillar and foundation for being able to develop new innovations in creating an expansion of the functions of Blockchain Technology in a wider sector.

Based on this analytical framework, BITFRIX (BTFX) aims to build a community of crypto enthusiasts that leverages the Polygon Ecosystem Token (POL) protocol to create synergies with other crypto communities; together, they support BITFRIX (BTFX) in developing a diverse infrastructure and a solid foundation, thereby enabling it to reach all segments of society.

BITFRIX (BTFX) subsequently developed a program planning framework that serves as the Master Plan for the development of the BITFRIX (BTFX) ecosystem, encompassing the following :

  1. Encourage Blockchain Enthusiast in the Polygon Ecosystem Token (POL) to provide full support to BITFRIX (BTFX) in developing infrastructure that supports some cross-community collaboration through the presence of BITFRIX (BTFX).
  2. Forming competent, creative and innovative human resources through education and training about how to use blockchain technology in various sectors of life to meet society's needs.
  3. Helping Small and Medium Enterprises to be able to adapt to current developments so that they are able to survive and have high competitiveness.
  4. Creating new breakthroughs in Blockchain Technology that can make it easier for people to understand the direction and development of technology so that they can compete and adapt widely with the global community in terms of digital financial technology.
  5. Develop Blockchain technology projects that involve the active participation of all community members to encourage the creation of strong, independent and technology-literate small and medium businesses.
  6. Connecting all blockchain and Cryptocurrency Enthusiast in one solid ecosystem chain through the development of the Cross-Network Protocol Program.
  7. Encourage non-profit organizations to collaborate and build partnerships with BITFRIX (BTFX) in adopting Blockchain technology as a technology standard used to build good and trusted management standards.
  8. Expanding the community activity model to build a stronger and more solid blockchain technology ecosystem..
